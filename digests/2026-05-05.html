Technical

  • Polkadot announced a new data storage model called Bulletin Chain, which enables on-chain data to have a default lifespan of around two weeks, with the option to extend, aiming to reduce the cost of permanent storage on decentralized networks (via 1, 2).
  • Storage access for Bulletin Chain will be managed through a quota system governed by the Polkadot community, with expired data remaining recoverable from archive nodes, ensuring no permanent data loss (via 3).
  • Bulletin Chain stores IPFS-compatible CIDs and guarantees data hosting, addressing a common limitation of IPFS where data persistence is not assured unless pinned (via 4).
  • The new storage model is positioned to support decentralized applications such as chats, social feeds, notifications, and file sharing, reducing reliance on centralized Web2 infrastructure (via 5).
  • Parity Technologies introduced ELVES, a research initiative for Polkadot and Kusama aimed at improving scalability by starting validation with a small subset of validators and expanding as needed, rather than requiring a full committee from the outset (via 6, 7).
