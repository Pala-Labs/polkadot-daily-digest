Technical

  • Parity Technologies introduced the concept of Bulletin Chain, described as a live data layer for applications such as chats, social feeds, notifications, session data, and lightweight web content. The posts emphasize that most internet data is short-lived and that existing Web3 storage solutions (IPFS, Arweave, Filecoin) focus on permanence, which does not match the needs of most consumer applications. Bulletin Chain is positioned as a solution for ephemeral, application-relevant data, allowing data to exist only as long as it is useful, rather than as permanent archives (via 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8).

  • jobyreuben announced a modular staking SDK for Substrate-based chains, aiming to eliminate the need for teams to rebuild staking logic when customizing rewards, penalties, or validator systems. The SDK includes components such as Pallet-Commitment (generalized bonding), Pallet-Authors (role system), Pallet-Chain-Manager (validator orchestration), Frame-Plugins (plugin models for rewards/penalties), Frame-Suite (type-driven runtime composition), and Pallet-XP (reputation layer). The approach is intended to reduce development time, complexity, and maintenance overhead by making staking configuration-driven rather than requiring protocol-level engineering (via 1).

Ecosystem

  • plaza.fun launched on Polkadot Hub mainnet, enabling users to create native pallet_assets tokens and trade them via a bonding curve mechanism. In the first week, 7 tokens were created (6 by community members), with approximately 100 trades and up to 9 holders per token. The platform features PlazaSwap (a Uniswap V2-style DEX), automatic graduation from bonding curve to DEX liquidity, LP burn after graduation, FeeKeyNFTs for creator revenue rights, and governance features such as timelock and emergency pause. The tokens are native runtime assets, XCM-ready, and not factory-deployed ERC-20 contracts. The team reports real mainnet usage with no marketing or incentives and is soliciting community feedback for further improvements (via 1).
