The post seeks input from runtime engineers and ecosystem teams before proposing further tooling improvements (forum).

  • Bulletin Chain storage mechanics: Parity Technologies announced that Bulletin Chain, a specialized storage chain in the Polkadot ecosystem, allows data submission via authorized extrinsics, indexing with Blake2b-256 hashes, and retrieval by content hash from IPFS or directly from the node. Stored data expires after a retention period unless renewed (x).
