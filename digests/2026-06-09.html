Technical

  • ⚡ The Polkadot community is currently voting on a major runtime upgrade to v2.3.0, which introduces a flexible minimum validator set size on Relay chains and lowers the default limits to 100 validators for Kusama and 250 for Polkadot. This change is part of the Dynamic Allocation Pool (DAP) roadmap, aiming to scale validator numbers based on demand, and includes an upgrade to the pallet recovery for improved account recovery options. The projected enactment date is around 15th June 2026 (via forum).
  • Multiple independent JAM implementations—including JAM Brains, JavaJAM, JamZig, FastRoll, and JAMneration—have successfully passed all stages of Milestone 1 conformance testing and fuzzing, confirming alignment with the JAM Gray Paper specifications under automated scrutiny.
