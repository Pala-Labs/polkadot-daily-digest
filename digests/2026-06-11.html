  • A community member has proposed the concept of SubWallet+, a product extension aiming to transform SubWallet into a European-focused, non-custodial super-app integrating Monerium, Acala, and Polkadot parachains.   • ⚡ Parity Technologies has released the final patch for the stable Polkadot SDK version stable2506, tagged as polkadot-stable2506-12 (also available as polkadot-v1.19.12).
