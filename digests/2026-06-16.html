Summary

Bifrost has proposed renewing its 1,000,000 DOT Treasury loan for a second year with new governance features for vDOT holders and a lower interest rate, while Snowbridge V1 is set for deprecation as users migrate to V2.

Report

Governance

  • ⚡ Bifrost has proposed to renew its 1,000,000 DOT Treasury loan for a second year, following the first year’s completion under Referendum #1538. The Year 1 loan generated 53,185.39 DOT in interest (5.318% APR), which Bifrost commits to repaying to the Polkadot Treasury by June 26th. The Year 2 renewal proposes a ~3% blended interest rate, with Bifrost taking no commission, and introduces new governance features allowing vDOT holders to participate in Polkadot OpenGov and delegate voting rights to BNC holders. The ~600,000 vDOT minted from the Year 2 loan will not participate in governance voting to avoid conflicts of interest (via forum).
  • dotID has been approved via OpenGov as registrar #5 on Polkadot (ref 1803) and #7 on Kusama (ref 626), providing free, end-to-end People Chain identity services. dotID recently audited both registries, flagging 978 accounts (336 on Polkadot, 642 on Kusama) as OutOfDate due to stale contact details, and encourages users and dApps to update and surface identity status accordingly (via forum).

Technical

  • Snowbridge V1 is set to be deprecated in favor of V2, which has been live since November 2025. The deprecation plan includes reducing V1 relaying to only profitable messages, rebalancing fee-paying accounts (with a proposed 1,000 DOT Treasury top-up and 500 DOT swap for ETH), increasing V1 fees after 30 days, and closing V1 channels after a 90-day migration period. V2 offers lower fees, off-chain fee estimation, unordered message execution, and improved XCM integration. Most bridge traffic has already migrated to V2, and remaining users are encouraged to transition promptly (via forum).
  • Parity Technologies continues work on a Rust SDK (cargo-pvm-contract) for writing smart contracts targeting the Polkadot Virtual Machine (PVM), with ongoing benchmarking of bytecode size and runtime performance (via x).

Ecosystem

The event will feature sessions on decentralised tools for journalism, free expression, and blockchain gaming, with participation from Polkadot Academy and other ecosystem projects (via x).
  • Polkadot has refreshed its brand to appeal to a broader audience, reflecting the ecosystem’s evolution beyond its original technical focus to include builders, artists, regulators, and gamers (via x).
