Vibly has finalized its core mechanism, introducing a non-liquidation stablecoin (VUSD) with governance deeply integrated into Polkadot OpenGov and transparent, treasury-driven token distribution. Report

Governance

  • Vibly has finalized its core mechanism with the introduction of a non-liquidation stablecoin (VUSD) and outlined a governance framework deeply integrated with Polkadot OpenGov. The team emphasizes transparent, treasury-driven governance, with no VIB reserved for the ArcheLabs team and most tokens held in the treasury or airdropped to DOT holders and early supporters. VUSD issuance, risk parameters, asset whitelists, oracles, and emergency handling will be subject to public governance, with limited modifications to OpenGov planned for Vibly’s specific needs (via Polkadot Forum Article).

Technical

  • At Web3 Summit, Dr. Gavin Wood and @byteboro from Parity discussed how JAM will provide Polkadot with collective compute capabilities, aiming to deliver Web2-scale products with Web3 primitives (via X).
  • Parity announced the Web3Summit testnet is open for developers to deploy and stress-test proof-of-concept repositories, encouraging experimentation with upstream code (via X).
  • Parity’s Humanity protocol was presented at Web3 Summit as a cryptographic, decentralized, and open authentication layer for proving humanness without revealing identity (via X).
  • JAM was highlighted for enabling continuous, indefinite execution and high-performance synchronous composition, surpassing the capabilities of traditional blockchains and parachains (via X).
  • A developer showcased a Proof of Delay app on Playground.dot, allowing users to verify and certify delays (e.g., with banks) on-chain using testnet infrastructure (via X).

Ecosystem

  • Vibly’s stablecoin system is designed to support both system and ecosystem projects, allowing permissionless participation and integration with DOT, Asset Hub, DeFi infrastructure, wallets, exchanges, and oracles. Third-party tokens can be converted into VIB/DOT/whitelisted assets to participate in VUSD generation (via Polkadot Forum Article).
