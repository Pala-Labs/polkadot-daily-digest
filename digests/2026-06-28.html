Report

Technical

  • Multiple independent teams are developing JAM protocol clients in different programming languages, with this client diversity explicitly positioned as a security feature for the network (via Polkadot Forum Article - OpenShore).
  • A proposal for $JAMKB, a fixed-supply resource token to meter JAM’s in-RAM state, has been clarified: $JAMKB is intended as a resource access token owned by the DOT DAO, not as a new currency competing with DOT. The JAM protocol requires such a token to limit the total amount of development on the network, ensuring soundness by strictly limiting resource usage. $JAMKB is not a cryptocurrency per se, but a protocol-level mechanism to manage access to JAM chain state (via Polkadot Forum Article - OpenShore, Medium Article - Gavin Wood).
  • JAM is described as a protocol generalising the Polkadot Relay-chain, enabling direct execution of code (including smart contracts, agents, and even regular software), and is backwards-compatible with the Relay-chain. JAM introduces more flexible and valuable coretime, with protocol improvements expected to reduce operational costs by about 40% and allow cores to be switched off when not economically viable (via Medium Article - Gavin Wood).

Ecosystem

  • Launchly, a multichain token launch platform previously active on Solana, BNB Chain, and Base, is now live on Polkadot Hub EVM. Trades are denominated in DOT, with 60% of fees allocated to creators (via Polkadot Forum Article - OpenShore).
  • Tokens held in contracts on Polkadot Asset Hub have doubled in the past 10 days, indicating a significant increase in TVL (Total Value Locked) (via Polkadot Forum Article - OpenShore).
  • A discussion highlights the potential for prediction markets like Polkamarkt to serve as DeFi primitives by integrating as pallets across parachains, DEXes, lending protocols, and DAOs, rather than remaining siloed as standalone applications (via Polkadot Forum Article - OpenShore).
  • The Robonomics / XRT Kusama migration has been delayed by 1–2 months to align with Kraken withdrawal timelines and provide the community with more preparation time (via Polkadot Forum Article - OpenShore).
