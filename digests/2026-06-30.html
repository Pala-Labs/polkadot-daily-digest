Report

Technical

  • A new proposal has been posted for a dynamic pricing mechanism for JAMKB, the proposed state-footprint access token for Polkadot JAM. The mechanism would use decaying deposits to ensure that holding state carries an ongoing cost, with prices rising as state becomes scarce and decaying deposits returning value to the DOT DAO. The proposal emphasizes that JAMKB is not a cryptocurrency but a resource right, and that protocol revenue from decayed deposits could be routed to activities that increase protocol value, such as buybacks or treasury accumulation. The authors invite community feedback and further simulation (via Polkadot Forum Article - kremena).

  • Polkadot SDK stable2603-4 patch released, delivering bug fixes, XCM improvements, performance optimisations, and security hardening across Snowbridge, XCMP Queue, Aura, and BEEFY (via BillLaboon, OpenShorex)
