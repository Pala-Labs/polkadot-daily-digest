Governance

  • Paseo testnet announced a major restructuring, with a forthcoming governance proposal to reduce its footprint to 20 cores (shared between System Chains and community parachains), supported by 60 validators. The changes include decommissioning Bridge Hub, Collectives, and Coretime chains on Paseo, a network reset (preserving Asset Hub, People, and Bulletin state), and shifting RPC provisioning to the Paseo bounty. The update cites the need for efficiency due to DOT price and evolving ecosystem priorities, with a leaner budget and possible short downtime during transition (via forum - PolkadotTestnet).
  • A public request was made to the Polkadot Community Foundation (PCF) for a detailed roadmap and full accounting of treasury funds allocated to the Polkadot App, noting that Referendum 1573 sent roughly 1.03M USDT to PCF (with 748,800 USD for software development), but as of mid-2026, the app is not yet available to general users and lacks public milestones or a launch date (via forum - thewhiterabbitM).
  • Referendum 1909 passed with 99.9% Aye, enacting zero validator commission, validator pay for self-stake, and permissionless chilling of underperformers. Two of three staking referenda are now live; Referendum 1910 (currently voting) would make nominators unslashable and reduce unbonding to 2 eras. Ref 1890 (enacted June 12) required validators to hold 10K DOT self-stake (via forum - OpenShore).
  • Bifrost is proposing to remove vDOT fees, with a governance vote pending to cut commission from 10% to 0% and unstake fee from 0.1% to 0%, allowing vDOT holders to receive 100% of staking rewards at zero cost (via forum - OpenShore).
  • dotID has requested registrar authority on Kusama People Chain, aiming to expand its on-chain identity hub beyond Polkadot mainnet (via forum - OpenShore).
