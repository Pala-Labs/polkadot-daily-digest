Governance

  • The multi-asset bounty is now on-chain, enabling governance to create bounties denominated in assets other than DOT. Additionally, a referendum is open to change the primary salary asset for contributors (via Polkadot Forum Article - Doordashcon).
  • Multiple Fellowship promotions were announced: Josep M Sobrepere (Polkadot-API lead) to Rank 2, Alin Dima to Rank 3, and Victor Oliva to Rank 2 (via Polkadot Forum Article - Doordashcon).

Technical

  • The Security Working Group reported ongoing fuzzing of runtime calls and XCM transfers to catch memory and invariant issues, with AI-assisted auditing proving effective enough that the group recommends expanding its use. An incident response plan is being developed, and runtime disclosures are tracked in a members-only repository (via Polkadot Forum Article - Doordashcon).
  • RFC #171 — Asset-Based Storage Deposit Payments was discussed, proposing to allow users to pay storage deposits in assets other than DOT. A proof of concept exists, and the group agreed that migrating pallets off hardcoded Currency/hold-mutate deposits onto the Consideration trait is a necessary architectural step (via Polkadot Forum Article - Doordashcon).
  • polkadot-sdk#11109 (migrating pallet-treasury from Currency to Fungible and deprecating spendlocal) and polkadot-sdk#11524 (adding a slashsubmission_deposit extrinsic to burn stuck submission deposits) are both ready for review and considered blockers for further pallet migrations (via Polkadot Forum Article - Doordashcon).
  • A temporary bug affecting validator set rotation is delaying reward distribution for native staking. Bifrost notes the vDOT/DOT exchange rate may remain static for a few days and redemptions may take longer, but no rewards are being lost (via Polkadot Forum Article - OpenShore).
  • SubWallet published an explainer on the JAM upgrade, highlighting how it addresses single-threaded transaction processing bottlenecks that can cause congestion during dApp usage spikes (via Polkadot Forum Article - OpenShore).
  • Commentary from ecosystem participants frames Parity Technologies as a technical provider and enabler, emphasizing their engineering capability rather than a governance role (via Polkadot Forum Article - OpenShore; X Posts - paritytech).
