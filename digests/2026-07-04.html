Technical

  • The Security Working Group reported ongoing fuzzing of runtime calls and XCM transfers to catch memory and invariant issues, with AI-assisted auditing proving effective enough that the group recommends expanding its use. An incident response plan is being developed, and runtime disclosures are tracked in a members-only repository (via Polkadot Forum Article - Doordashcon).
  • RFC #171 — Asset-Based Storage Deposit Payments was discussed, proposing to allow users to pay storage deposits in assets other than DOT. A proof of concept exists, and the group agreed that migrating pallets off hardcoded Currency/hold-mutate deposits onto the Consideration trait is a necessary architectural step (via Polkadot Forum Article - Doordashcon).
  • polkadot-sdk#11109 (migrating pallet-treasury from Currency to Fungible and deprecating spendlocal) and polkadot-sdk#11524 (adding a slashsubmission_deposit extrinsic to burn stuck submission deposits) are both ready for review and considered blockers for further pallet migrations (via Polkadot Forum Article - Doordashcon).
