Technical

  • MiniJAM, a streamlined version of JAM, will launch as a Polkadot parachain, preserving core JAM mechanisms (PVM, service, refine, accumulate) while replacing JAM's consensus with Polkadot parachain security and a MiniJAM worker validation mechanism. MiniJAM will use a native token, MINI, for security and incentives, and aims for seamless migration to the JAM mainnet, after which it will operate as an L2 on JAM. The testnet is planned within 1–2 months and mainnet within 3–4 months, with a focus on tooling compatibility, governance minimization, and innovative incentive mechanisms. The team has completed the core on-chain part and will provide further updates, including on JAMKB solutions, ahead of the testnet launch (via Polkadot Forum Article - libingjiang47).
