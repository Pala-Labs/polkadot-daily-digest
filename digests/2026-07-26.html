Governance

  • ultracoconut proposed removing the Bounties pallet from the Polkadot runtime, arguing that bounties add governance complexity, lock Treasury capital for long periods, and offer limited advantages over direct Treasury funding through OpenGov. The proposed changes include deprecating the pallet, closing all active bounties, returning remaining funds to the Treasury, removing associated on-chain storage, and excluding the pallet from future runtime releases.
