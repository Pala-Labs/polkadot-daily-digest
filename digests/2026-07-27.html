  • Onepebble argued that the separate proposal to remove the bounties pallet addresses accountability problems more than pallet-specific problems, and proposed a minimum standard for delegated treasury spending: on-chain scope and acceptance criteria before disbursement, recorded verification for each tranche, mandatory delays for large releases, identifiable proposing entities, and reputational or financial consequences for approvers. The post also discloses that Two Pebbles Ventures has built five FRAME pallets live on Paseo testnet for milestone definitions, escrow, release delays, and curator reputation, and asks for a bounded pilot on Small and Medium Spender tracks.
