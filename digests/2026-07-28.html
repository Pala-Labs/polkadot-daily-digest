Technical

  • RFC 174 proposes a light-client request for extrinsics-trie inclusion proofs: instead of downloading a full block body, a client would receive the trie hashes needed to recompute the extrinsics root and check whether tracked transactions were included. The write-up says an implementation is already open against polkadot-sdk #12644, with a dedicated RPC method and client-library updates planned after the RFC lands. (via Polkadot Forum Article - Doordashcon)
  • RFC 173 addresses transaction replay after an account is reaped and later refunded, causing its nonce to reset to zero. Josep M Sobrepere’s preferred design adds a CheckCreatedAtHeight transaction extension carrying the account creation height alongside the explicit nonce, while the call also discussed Oliver Tale-Yazdi’s earlier runtimes#248 approach and migration concerns for roughly three million existing accounts and offline signers. (via Polkadot Forum Article - Doordashcon)
