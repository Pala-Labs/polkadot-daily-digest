Governance

Technical

  • Two RFCs were reviewed: RFC 174 would let light clients verify transaction inclusion from extrinsics-trie proofs instead of full block bodies (implementation already open as polkadot-sdk #12644), and RFC 173 would stop replay attacks on reaped-then-refunded accounts whose nonce resets to zero, via a CheckCreatedAtHeight extension — with migration concerns raised for roughly three million existing accounts and offline signers. (via Polkadot Forum Article - Doordashcon)
