Governance

  • ultracoconut proposed creating a permanent, cryptographically inaccessible Polkadot burn account as an implementation path for Wish For Change #1926, which the post says proposes burning DOT revenue generated by JAMKB. The argument is that sending DOT to an unspendable account would remove it from circulation while leaving total issuance unchanged under the monetary model targeting approximately 2.1 billion DOT maximum issuance. Ecosystem

  • FlowFarm said it has paused its previously proposed Polkadot-native perpetual-markets parachain after being unable to secure the funding, liquidity, audits, oracle infrastructure, and operating capacity it considered necessary for a responsible launch. The project is now focusing on non-custodial DOT and KSM staking infrastructure, including nomination-pool infrastructure, wallet-approved staking and migration flows, finalized-chain data, historical explorers, and monitored migration assistance. (via Polkadot Forum Article - flowfarm)

  • FlowFarm also announced a limited migration reward-protection promotion: eligible monitored DOT or KSM migrations must reach finalized Complete status by August 23, 2026 at 23:59:59 UTC, with verified reimbursements sent manually by August 30, 2026. The reimbursement estimate is based on migrated principal, the destination FlowFarm pool’s finalized trailing-30-day annualized reward rate, and finalized unbonding duration divided by 365.25 days. (via Polkadot Forum Article - flowfarm)

  • Endstate, described by its builder as a game on Polkadot, is seeking community feedback before testnet on its supply-chain simulation design, where players manage tribes across a ringworld map with hex tiles, biomes, resources, cell roles, and on-chain event-driven gameplay. The post says the rules are implemented in a crate designed around on-chain constraints, with a tile turn taking around 100 microseconds. (via Polkadot Forum Article - polykrate)
