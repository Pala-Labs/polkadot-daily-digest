polkadot{.js} extension 0.64.0 was released to fix a signing-prompt defect that could hide the real transaction, with users advised to update. A forum post also raised concerns about concentration of moderator and curator roles.

Report

Governance

  • A forum post argued that moderator and curator responsibilities are becoming concentrated among a small group, saying four individuals collectively receive $12,150 per month from curator and moderation roles while holding positions across almost all remaining active bounties; the post calls for discussion of curator rotation, limits on simultaneous roles, and conflict-of-interest guidelines. (via Polkadot Forum Article - Megadot)

Technical

  • polkadot{.js} extension 0.64.0 fixes a signing-prompt defect affecting all versions before 0.64.0, where a connected site could obtain a signature over a transaction while the prompt displayed an inert off-chain message; the fix was published to the Chrome Web Store and addons.mozilla.org on 31 July 2026, and the post says maintainers are not aware of any exploitation. Users are advised to update to 0.64.0 or later, and wallets derived from the codebase are advised to verify whether their signing UI is derived from payload contents rather than the request channel. (via Polkadot Forum Article - patricio)
