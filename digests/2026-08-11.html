  • ZkRefine is presented as MiniJAM’s next stage, replacing reliance on nodes replaying Work Packages with zero-knowledge proofs that provide cryptographic guarantees for Refine correctness. The post says the team is considering both translation to existing zkVM instruction sets and a native PVM proving backend, with trade-offs between engineering effort and proving efficiency. (via Polkadot Forum Article - libingjiang47)

  • The next ZkRefine milestone, Smoke, is estimated at 7–10 days and aims to implement the complete Refine flow in the simplest form, including proof relations, core Host Calls, Memory and Gas semantics, consistency testing against native Refine, and performance baselines. (via Polkadot Forum Article - libingjiang47)

  • The post also frames ZkRefine as potentially reusable by a future Kusama, arguing that it could support “extreme decentralization” by reducing the need for validators to replay full Work Packages and enabling validator groups composed of resource-constrained workers, coordinators, storage nodes, and proof-generation hardware. (via Polkadot Forum Article - libingjiang47)

  • In the MiniJAM recap, MINI Genesis is reported as launched, Mini Lucky is described as largely complete but being reworked with an expected opening “within the next couple of days,” and the Incentive Market core implementation is described as largely complete with testnet launch listed under next steps.
