Governance

  • Unified Collator Pool for System Chains was proposed as a shared pool of 64 active operators for Polkadot Hub, Bridge Hub, People, Collectives, and other System Chains, replacing separate collator sets with protocol-assigned block production across one operator pool. The proposal includes permissionless entry subject to hardware, connectivity, and self-bond requirements, automatic chilling for persistent underperformance, and an indicative Treasury budget of approximately $300,000 USD equivalent per year, or about $4,700 per slot per year. (via Polkadot Forum Article - ultracoconut)

  • WFC: Completing the #1710 Monetary Reform was posted as a draft for discussion, not yet a formal referendum proposal, arguing that the issuance reduction from roughly 120M DOT to approximately 55.8M DOT with a long-term supply cap of 2.1B DOT should be extended by reducing structural DOT sell pressure. The proposal calls for restoring Treasury burns, setting a Treasury ceiling at 1.5% of circulating DOT supply, burning 0.5% of the Treasury balance per burn period, lowering minimum validator commission from 10% to 2%, and funding validator self-stake incentives primarily from real protocol revenue rather than permanent inflationary allocations. (via Polkadot Forum Article - coco)
