Governance

  • Kusama JAM-era futures: emielsebastiaan published a discussion draft for KSM DAO outlining five possible paths for Kusama in the JAM era, including status quo downsizing, DOT/KSM merger or acquisition, lightweight or low-latency Kusama JAM, full Kusama JAM, and Kusama/Polkadot cohabitation on one JAM. The paper says it does not request funds, authorize migration, or supersede WFC #573, and frames cohabitation—potentially backed by DOT DAO’s staking economy—as needing formal technical, economic, and constitutional study. (via Polkadot Forum Article - emielsebastiaan)

  • The draft argues Kusama faces a structural security-cost issue after Referendum #655, citing 700 validators and a working assumption of $3,000 per validator per month, or $2.1 million per month and $25.2 million per year, against a referenced $55.1 million KSM market capitalization on 12 August 2026. The authors stress this is a stress test, not a claim about current inflation or validator payouts. (via Polkadot Forum Article - emielsebastiaan)
