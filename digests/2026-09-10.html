Polkadot Daily Digest: 2026-09-10

Summary

Polkadot Product Devnet is rolling out a major update with new mobile and desktop apps, requiring users to back up recovery phrases, reinstall, and create new usernames while losing contacts/messages; DotNS and the CDM registry also moved to new contracts.

Report

Technical

  • Polkadot Product Devnet is rolling out a major update alongside new mobile and desktop apps; users are told to back up recovery phrases if they want to retain access to published .dot domains, delete backups, uninstall, and reinstall, with the update requiring new usernames and causing loss of contacts/messages. (via Polkadot Forum Article - pcf_network, X Posts - Polkadot, X Posts - paritytech)

  • The Devnet update changes chat encryption from P-256 to X25519, updates the cryptographic context for personhood pseudonyms, and redeploys the CDM registry to fix failures when publishing new contract names. Desktop adds audio/video calls and chat search; mobile adds chat search, a new tab bar UI, transfer replies inside chat, improved multimedia downloads, an audio-device picker, registration progress states, error-message improvements, coinage-management improvements, and improved PGAS. (via Polkadot Forum Article - pcf_network)

  • DotNS has migrated to a new set of contracts: every .dot name was migrated, but app records were recreated from a snapshot and may need republishing if they fail to load or show older versions. Developers are told to use @parity/polkadot-app-deploy@latest with pad 0.16.1 or later and @polkadot-community-foundation/dotns-cli@latest with 0.9.1 or later, because older releases still point at retired DotNS contracts. (via Polkadot Forum Article - pcf_network)

  • The CDM contract registry is moving to 0x05662b3dbd5dd9f2ff92d67630477e84b0b37c1f; developers should update @polkadot-community-foundation/cdm-cli and @polkadot-community-foundation/cdm-env, regenerate cdm.json, and avoid registering new contract names on the old registry. The old registry remains readable after the switch, but anything registered there after the snapshot will not appear in the new registry. (via Polkadot Forum Article - pcf_network)

  • After runtime upgrades land, developers need to regenerate and re-pin chain descriptors for Bulletin, Asset Hub, and People, because stale descriptors will fail at decode. The Bulletin authorization structure changes shape, with bytesPermanent moving from the top level into a nested extra field; published data is not affected, only how it is read. (via Polkadot Forum Article - pcf_network)
