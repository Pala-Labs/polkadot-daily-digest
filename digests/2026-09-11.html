Polkadot Daily Digest: 2026-09-11

Summary

A new Road to JAM update outlined moving parachain finality to a JAM service and a Minimal Relay end state with zero DOT on the Relay Chain. Governance also saw a live dotUSD referendum and a $310k stablecoin transfer to the current Treasury account.

Report

Governance

  • A referendum proposing dotUSD, described as a protocol-native stablecoin for Polkadot, is live on DOT DAO governance; Polkadot said it was drafted by community members and submitted to OpenGov via the Polkadot Community Foundation. (via X Posts - Polkadot)
  • The Polkadot Treasury moved about $310k in USDC/USDT from the legacy Treasury account on Asset Hub to the current Treasury account using the permissionless ahOps.transferToPostMigrationTreasury extrinsic. Additional stablecoins can be routed to the Treasury but will require governance action. (via Polkadot Forum Article - bill_w3f)

Technical

  • A new “Road to JAM” update outlines the planned switchover of parachain finality to a JAM service, covering PolkaJAM, Minimal Relay / AHM v2, Offchain Messaging, and Parachain Service. The stated end state for Minimal Relay is “zero DOT on the Relay Chain,” no user-callable transactions, and no way to move funds onto it again, with parachain registration, channel management, and on-demand coretime ordering moving to the Coretime chain. (via Polkadot Forum Article - joyce)
  • The same JAM roadmap says PolkaJAM will be a full node implementation following Gray Paper v1.0, with an in-browser light-node library, while Offchain Messaging is intended as an alternative to on-chain HRMP and targets future latency reductions to “100s of milliseconds.” (via Polkadot Forum Article - joyce)
  • Parity said it is rebuilding Polkadot Dart “from the ground up,” with a light-client-first approach using smoldot’s Rust implementation, audited crypto crates replacing Dart-native operations, and Trinity plus Product SDK support via Rust. (via X Posts - paritytech)

Ecosystem

  • PoUC Network 1.0 was introduced as an independent open-source experiment based on the Polkadot SDK, explicitly not an official Polkadot project, parachain, or endorsed integration. Its repository includes runtime pallets, a TypeScript SDK and API clients, AI safety/red-team and evaluation DApps, an Android edge-node app, documentation, and local testnet workflows; the author says version 1.0 does not require a native token or staking. (via Polkadot Forum Article - pouc)
