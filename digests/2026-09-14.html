Report

Governance

  • Decidimos is seeking community feedback before posting a Medium Spender preimage for US$190,000 over six months, payable in stablecoins on Asset Hub or the DOT equivalent, to build a Polkadot payment rail for Potosí lithium-revenue distribution. The forum post says the project could bring about US$104 million a year in stablecoin settlement, US$4.4 billion over its life, more than 10 million transactions a year, and one-month resting balances of about US$15 million, with payments tied to 861,292 residents and community works; planned deliverables include a gasless smart-account wallet on Polkadot Hub, batched payout engine, cédula-based recovery, Bolivian cash-out corridor, audits, and open-source contracts. (via Polkadot Forum Article - Decedimos)

Ecosystem

  • Relaycode v0.3.0 expands from a type-aware extrinsic builder into a broader browser-based developer platform with Relaycode Studio, a smart-contract workspace for Polkadot Hub that supports multi-file Solidity editing, EVM or PVM compilation, OpenZeppelin imports, gas and fee estimates, Polkadot wallet signing, deployment, and movement between supported Asset Hub networks. The release also refreshes the Extrinsic Builder with updated Dedot, LunoKit, Next.js, React and TypeScript foundations, restored signing and submission on Polkadot Asset Hub, improved wallet startup and RPC fallback behaviour, Paseo support, clearer transaction-status handling, and more than 1,000 tests. (via Polkadot Forum Article - itsyogesh)
